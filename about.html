Our Vision

Australian Standards, Canadian Commitment

Ralph started Polish Perfect Floors with one clear goal: bring the quality, attention to detail and professional standards of Australian hardwood floor restoration to the Canadian community.

We’re a new Canadian business. We’re not new to the craft.

We believe every hardwood floor can look beautiful again. So before anyone talks replacement, we talk restoration: professional sanding, staining and refinishing that restores natural character, enhances appearance and extends the life of the floor you already have.
