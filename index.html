Bring new life to your old floor

Ralph Samad has helped manage and deliver more than 500 hardwood floor projects across Victoria, Australia. Now he brings those Australian flooring standards to homes in Canada.

Stains and clear coats are chosen for depth, colour and durable protection. Each finish is matched to the home it goes into.

Careful prep comes first, then even sanding with clean edges and corners. We work with dust in mind and inspect the floor before each coat.

At the end of the job, we walk the floor together. It is not finished until you are happy with it.
